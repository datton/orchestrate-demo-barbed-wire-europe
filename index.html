Barbed Wire Europe

A short, careful directory of European barbed wire manufacturers, with plain-English background on what barbed wire is and how to choose it.

What this directory covers

  • Manufacturers: European makers, filterable by country.
  • About barbed wire: what it is, a short history and the main types.
  • How to choose: practical questions to ask before you buy.
  • Sources: where the information comes from and how we handled gaps.

Our rule on facts

Only details we are certain of are listed. If a manufacturer detail could not be confirmed, it is left out rather than guessed, so the directory is short on purpose. Always confirm products and specifications with the manufacturer.
